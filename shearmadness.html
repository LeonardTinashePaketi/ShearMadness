Frequently Asked

Got questions?
We've got answers.

Do you accept walk-ins?
Absolutely — we're a walk-in-friendly shop. That said, booking ahead via Fresha or WhatsApp guarantees your slot and cuts your wait time dramatically, especially on Saturdays.
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Friday: 09:00 – 18:00. Saturday: 09:00 – 15:00. Sunday & Public Holidays: Closed.
Do you do ladies' hair and colour?
Yes — we're a full barbershop AND hair cafe. We offer ladies' wash, cut & blow, root touch-ups, full highlights, balayage, treatments and Olaplex repairs — all with Shireen.
Where exactly are you?
We're in The Avenues — Shop 5C+D, 1 Drama Street, Somerset West, 7130. Right next to Spoon Bakery and Proof Wine Bar.
What's the difference between booking on Fresha vs WhatsApp?
Fresha gives you instant confirmation and lets you choose your specific barber, date and time. WhatsApp is best for custom requests, quotes, kids' bookings or quick questions — we usually reply within minutes during shop hours.
